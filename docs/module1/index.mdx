# Module 1: Introduction to Physical AI

## Welcome to Physical AI

The future of AI extends beyond digital spaces into the physical world. This module introduces **Physical AI**—AI systems that function in reality, comprehend physical laws, and interact with the environment through embodied agents.

## What You'll Learn

In this module, you'll discover how AI transitions from purely digital computation to physical embodiment, enabling robots to perceive, reason about, and act in the real world.

### Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

✅ **Define Physical AI** and explain how it differs from traditional AI systems
✅ **Understand embodied intelligence** and why the physical body matters for intelligence
✅ **Identify challenges** unique to physical AI (sensing, acting, learning in reality)
✅ **Survey humanoid robotics** applications and understand why human form is advantageous
✅ **Navigate the tools ecosystem** including ROS 2, Gazebo, and NVIDIA Isaac Sim

## Module Structure

This module consists of 5 chapters:

### 1. What is Physical AI?
Learn the fundamental concepts of Physical AI and how it differs from digital AI systems you've worked with before.

### 2. Embodied Intelligence
Explore how physical bodies shape intelligence and why robots need to understand the physical world.

### 3. Challenges in Physical AI
Understand the unique difficulties of sensing, acting, and learning in the real world versus simulation.

### 4. Humanoid Robotics
Discover why human-shaped robots are valuable and survey current humanoid platforms.

### 5. Tools Overview
Get introduced to the essential tools: ROS 2 for robot control, Gazebo for simulation, and NVIDIA Isaac for advanced AI.

## Prerequisites

Before starting this module, you should have:

- **Basic AI/ML knowledge**: Familiarity with supervised learning, neural networks, and training concepts
- **Python programming**: Comfortable writing Python code and using libraries like NumPy
- **Computer science fundamentals**: Understanding of algorithms, data structures, and systems

:::tip No Robotics Experience Required!
This course is designed for software engineers and AI practitioners **without prior robotics experience**. We'll build your understanding from first principles.
:::
