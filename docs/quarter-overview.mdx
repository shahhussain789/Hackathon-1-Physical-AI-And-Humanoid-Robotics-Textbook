# Quarter Overview

The future of AI extends beyond digital spaces into the physical world. This capstone quarter introduces **Physical AI**—AI systems that function in reality and comprehend physical laws. Students learn to design, simulate, and deploy humanoid robots capable of natural human interactions using ROS 2, Gazebo, and NVIDIA Isaac.

## What You'll Learn

Over this quarter, you will:

- **Understand Physical AI**: Grasp the fundamental differences between traditional AI and AI systems operating in the physical world
- **Master Robot Control**: Learn kinematics, dynamics, and control theory for humanoid robots
- **Develop Perception Systems**: Implement computer vision, sensor fusion, and SLAM for robot perception
- **Apply AI to Locomotion**: Use reinforcement learning and classical methods for bipedal walking and manipulation
- **Deploy Real Systems**: Bridge simulation to reality with practical deployment strategies

## Course Structure

The course is organized into 4 progressive modules:

### Module 1: Introduction to Physical AI & Embodied Intelligence
Foundations of Physical AI, embodied intelligence, and the unique challenges of real-world robotics.

### Module 2: Humanoid Control Fundamentals
Robot kinematics, dynamics, and control theory with hands-on Python and ROS 2 examples.

### Module 3: Perception & Sensor Fusion for Humanoids
Computer vision, SLAM, and sensor fusion techniques for humanoid robot perception.

### Module 4: AI for Humanoid Locomotion & Manipulation
Reinforcement learning and classical methods for bipedal walking, manipulation, and human-robot interaction.

## Technologies & Tools

- **ROS 2 Humble**: Robot Operating System for middleware and integration
- **Gazebo**: Physics-based robot simulator
- **NVIDIA Isaac Sim**: Advanced GPU-accelerated simulation platform
- **Python 3.10+**: Primary programming language for AI/ML implementations
- **PyTorch/TensorFlow**: Deep learning frameworks for neural network training

## Prerequisites

- **CS/AI Background**: Undergraduate-level computer science and AI/ML knowledge
- **Python Programming**: Proficiency in Python
- **Mathematics**: Linear algebra, calculus, basic probability theory
- **No Robotics Experience Required**: This course bridges from traditional AI to physical robotics

## Learning Approach

This interactive book provides:

- **Progressive Complexity**: Student-friendly explanations before formal terminology
- **Hands-On Code**: Complete, executable Python/ROS 2 examples you can run locally
- **Interactive Visualizations**: 3D robot models and simulations embedded in the browser
- **Self-Assessment**: End-of-chapter exercises with solutions
- **Curated Resources**: Links to research papers, datasets, and community forums
